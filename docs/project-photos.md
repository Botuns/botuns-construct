# Completed bungalow photography

Source: owner-supplied Google Photos album, https://photos.app.goo.gl/tp4V765X6kk5Jxf4A

The owner described this as a recently completed bungalow on 9 October 2026. No precise completion date or project location has been supplied.

Six photos of the grey-and-cream bungalow and its interiors were downloaded and converted to WebP for local serving. No Google account connection or remote image URLs are required by the site. The red-roof exterior photos in the album were not used because they appear to show a different building.
